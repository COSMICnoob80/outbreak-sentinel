Every early-warning system has the same flaw: it never keeps score. Anyone can print a red alert. Nobody comes back a week later to say whether they were right.

Outbreak Sentinel fixes that. It scores news, weather, and social chatter with a deterministic rule into a calibrated risk card — and a city with no city-specific report is capped at LOW, so weather alone can never escalate it. Then it files a signed receipt: the sha256 of the claim, appended to an immutable ledger.

New this build: it does not call APIs directly. It buys ranked intelligence through Telegraph, one paid request routed to a miner, and the answer it buys becomes a cited driver on the card. Settlement on Base Sepolia. The money is on screen: miner, amount, tx hash, network.

A week later we resolve the receipt against a real source, confirmed or refuted. The scoreboard is the honest track record. "Trust our AI" becomes "check our ledger."
